# AI domain: acceptance record

Plan: `2026-11-01-ai-domain.md`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-ai-domain-design.md` §9. Branch `ai-domain`, verified 2026-11-01 on macOS with Claude Code, the pack loaded from this worktree with `--plugin-dir`, no MCP servers (`--strict-mcp-config --mcp-config '{"mcpServers":{}}'`), `--allowedTools Read,Write,Edit,Glob,Grep,Skill,Bash --max-turns 40`, every prompt ending "I won't be around to answer questions".

Data, all synthetic and generated by a seeded script:
- `outputs.csv`: 100 answers from a support bot. 12 invent a refund or credit policy, 8 are rude, 3 echo an `@example.com` email and an account number, and 77 are fine.
- `judge-labels.csv`: the eval fixture. 40 rows: 33 human passes, all passed by the judge; 7 human fails, of which the judge passed 6.
- `judge-small.csv`: 5 rows.
