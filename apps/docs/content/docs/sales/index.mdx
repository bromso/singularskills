The Sales domain works one deal at a time: qualify, discovery, solution, proposal, close plan and review. Each phase writes a numbered file to `.singularskills/sales/<deal>/` with the decisions made and the questions still open. Six utilities define the ideal customer profile, prepare a call, draft a follow-up, answer one objection, review the pipeline, and find why many deals were won or lost.

Every buyer fact is labelled **known** (with its source), **assumed** or **unknown**, and every person **identified** or **engaged**. Numbers are `computed` from the buyer's or your own figures, or labelled an `illustration`. No draft uses false urgency, fake scarcity or misrepresentation, and nothing beyond your stated policy is offered: it is marked **for approval**.

## Sales Phases

| Phase | Command | Purpose |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Qualify | `/sales-qualify` | Fit, MEDDPICC, people, the risk of no decision; pursue, nurture or drop |
| Discovery | `/sales-discovery` | What the buyer said on a held call, in their words |
| Solution | `/sales-solution` | The value case from the buyer's numbers, and the proof plan |
| Proposal | `/sales-proposal` | Options priced from your policy |
| Close plan | `/sales-close-plan` | The mutual action plan to go-live |
| Review | `/sales-deal-review` | Why one deal was won or lost, from both sides |

## What the Research Says

- Listening and adapting drive sales outcomes (Itani et al., 2019).
- Sellers misjudge why they lose; buyers name adaptability, relationship and cost, and "price" is often the easy answer (Friend et al., 2014; Clozd; Corporate Visions).
- Complex deals are decided by a committee, and often by no one: "no decision" is the competitor to plan for.

## Cross-Domain Integration

- **Marketing** owns the team's battle cards and objection guide (`/marketing-sales-enablement`), cold outbound, pipeline design and pricing; the sales skills read marketing's customer research and pricing work.
- **Legal** owns contract review and negotiation prep; the close plan links them.
