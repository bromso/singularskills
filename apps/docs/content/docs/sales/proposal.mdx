## Purpose

The Proposal phase drafts what the buyer receives, opening with their situation in their words.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/sales-proposal northwind
```

## Prerequisites

Requires `.singularskills/sales/<deal>/02-discovery.md` and `03-solution.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Gets the pricing and discount policy</Step>
<Step>Opens with the buyer's situation</Step>
<Step>Sets scope and outcomes tied to their metrics</Step>
<Step>Offers two or three options within policy</Step>
<Step>Marks anything beyond policy for approval</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/sales/<deal>/04-proposal.md` containing:

- Proposal draft
- For approval
</Output>
