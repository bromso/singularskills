## Scenario: Northwind Freight

You sell route-planning software. Northwind Freight, a 300-truck haulier, filled in a demo request. Their ops director, Priya, mentioned that planners spend most of Monday rebuilding routes by hand.

### Phase 1: Qualify

```
/sales-qualify northwind
```

**Sample output snippet:**
> **Economic buyer:** unknown. Priya is engaged, but the CFO has not been named. **No decision:** if they do nothing, planners keep losing Mondays; cost unknown until discovery.
> **Recommendation: pursue**, if the CFO can be identified on the first call.

### Phase 2: Discovery

```
/sales-discovery northwind
```

**Sample output snippet:**
> **Pain (Priya, ops director):** "Every Monday four planners rebuild the week by hand, and we still miss delivery windows."
> **Metrics:** 4 planners × 8 hours a week (known, call of 12 May).

### Phases 3–5

```
/sales-solution northwind
/sales-proposal northwind
/sales-close-plan northwind
```

**Sample output snippet:**
> **Value case (computed):** 32 planner-hours a week at £38 an hour is about £63,000 a year, from Priya's figures.
> **For approval:** Priya asked for 25% off; policy allows 15%. The proposal shows 15% and the rest needs the sales director.
> **Close plan:** go-live 1 September (buyer's date); security review starts now, in parallel with the commercial decision.

### Phase 6: Review

```
/sales-deal-review northwind
```

### Across many deals

```
/sales-win-loss lost-deals.csv
```

> **Price check:** reps logged "price" on 14 of 20 losses, but buyer debriefs on 8 of those name slow responses, missing integrations or a champion who left. Cutting prices is not supported by this evidence.
