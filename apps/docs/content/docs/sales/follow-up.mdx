## Purpose

The Follow-up skill drafts the recap: what you heard, what was agreed, and the next step, promising nothing the notes do not support.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/sales-follow-up [deal] <notes>
```

## Prerequisites

The call notes.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Restates the buyer's situation in their words</Step>
<Step>Lists what was agreed</Step>
<Step>Proposes the next step with a date</Step>
<Step>Flags anything unclear</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Returns the email in chat. Writes no file.
</Output>
